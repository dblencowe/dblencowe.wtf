I am Dave Blencowe (dblencowe), based in Manchester, a cyber security engineer & tech enthusiast.

Most of what I make starts with me wondering whether I could build it myself. Right now that means games in Unreal Engine, a homelab with its own server rack (and LEDs on the door that turn red when something breaks), a desk display made out of an old Mycroft smart speaker, and a steady stream of 3D prints for around the flat. When a job needs wood rather than plastic, I get the saw out.

This site is my notebook for all of it. I write things up so I can remember how I did them, and in case they save someone else an evening of trial and error. Posts are grouped by [project](/projects/), and the [skills page](/skills/) shows what each one covers.

## The day job

I'm a principal security engineer at [watchTowr](https://watchtowr.com). Before that I built and ran platforms at a fintech, in the motor trade and at a run of agencies, and led the teams doing it. The curiosity behind the side projects is what I bring to work now, and the two feed each other more than I expected. If you want the formal version, it's on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/dblencowe).
